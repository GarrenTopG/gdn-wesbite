Practical community support, rooted in Kuils River.

A local milestone

A place for young readers in Sunbird Park

In July 2022, Aurorah CAN established a community mini-library in Sunbird Park as part of Nelson Mandela Day. It reflects the network’s focus on practical, community-led support for young people.
