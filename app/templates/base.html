Privacy & POPIA notice

Aurorah NPC, at 27 Lovebird Walk, Sunbird Park, Cape Town, 7580, is responsible for personal information submitted through this site. We use it to assess assistance requests, review volunteer applications, record donation pledges or offers, and contact you about those submissions. Information is available to authorised Aurorah CAN staff and, where needed to review an assistance request, relevant community partners. Required fields are identified on each form; if you do not provide them, we may be unable to assess or respond to your submission. Please do not include sensitive details that are not needed. You may ask to access, correct or delete your information in person at the address above during its published hours. You may also complain to South Africa's Information Regulator at inforeg@justice.gov.za.
