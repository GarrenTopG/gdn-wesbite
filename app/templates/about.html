About Aurorah CAN

Aurorah CAN is the community action network of Aurorah NPC, a non-profit company working alongside vulnerable households and young people in Kuils River and nearby Cape Flats communities.

Our story

Community action shaped by local needs

Founded in Kuils River by community activist, author and human-rights advocate Latifa Jacobs, Aurorah CAN grew from grassroots community action during the COVID-19 pandemic. Its work focuses on building bridges between residents and community organisations, addressing hunger, supporting young people and contributing to community development.

The network's approach is grounded in local relationships and participation. Its work has included food relief, educational support, community engagement and advocacy on issues including gender-based violence and social inclusion.

Founder

Latifa Jacobs

Jacobs' work includes participatory community research and advocacy on gender-based violence, femicide and social inclusion. Her community bridge-building has received recognition, including the Brand South Africa Play Your Part Award; she was also named a finalist in the Good Hope FM Warrior Woman Awards.

Recognition and award details are included here as background to the organisation's story, not as a claim of government endorsement.

Work in the community

Practical support and community development

Aurorah CAN brings local residents, volunteers and community partners together around needs in the area. Support is considered according to the circumstances of each request and the resources available.

Food relief

Grassroots efforts to address hunger and connect households with local support.

Youth and learning

Educational support and community-led opportunities for young people.

Community development

Building relationships between residents, civil-society groups and local initiatives.

A local milestone

Sunbird Park community mini-library

In July 2022, Aurorah CAN established a community mini-library in Sunbird Park for Nelson Mandela Day. This milestone reflects the organisation's focus on practical community-led support for young people.

How support is coordinated

Requests and offers submitted through this site are recorded for review. Authorised staff assess needs and available resources, and may coordinate with community partners where appropriate. This website does not provide automated verification, live donor tracking or instant allocation updates.
